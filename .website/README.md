A 2D portal puzzler. The unicorn's horn fires the cold portal, its tail
fires the warm one. Portals stick to clouds like wet paint — never to stone.

The catch: the horn aims through a 130° arc, the tail through a 150° arc,
and they don't overlap — 79° of the circle belongs to neither. To place both
ends of a wormhole you have to turn the horse around. Every puzzle is built
on that.

Momentum carries through, and every transit multiplies it by 1.12. Drop into
a floor portal, come out of a wall portal faster than you went in — then loop
the pair and build speed until it caps.

21 levels. Nothing is explained in words except the controls: every rule is
taught by the geometry of the room — ink pools, patrolling shades, anvils that
can only be moved by dropping them, prisms that strip portals off, and lasers
you can bend through your own wormhole.

Theme: colour is the treasure, paint is the weapon, colourlessness is the enemy.

Desktop — A/D or arrows move, Space/W/Up jumps, E grabs, R restarts, M mutes,
left click fires the cold portal, right click the warm one.
Mobile — on-screen controls, tap to aim.

Single file, no external resources. All audio synthesised at runtime — no
sample files. Sound engine: ZzFX by Frank Force (MIT).
Rainbow contrast flicker kept within WCAG 2.3.1 area limits.
